Author FAQ

Who can sell on AppShuk?

Anyone who makes an app for the Jewish community: teachers and morahs, parents, developers, shuls, schools, businesses and organizations. You don't need to be a company.

What does it cost to list?

Nothing up front, and free apps list free.

Stripe’s processing fee (about 3% + 30¢) comes off each sale. AppShuk keeps 15%. The lowest price is $1.99. Example: on a $14.99 app you receive about $12.00.

How do I get paid?

Through Stripe. Buyers pay by card, and Stripe sends your share to your bank account. You'll set up a free Stripe account when your app is approved.

What do you check before an app goes live?

That it works, that it does what the description says, and that the content is kosher and right for a Jewish home, school or shul. If something needs changing we'll tell you what.

What kind of apps can I list?

Apps that open in a web browser, on a computer, tablet, phone or smartboard, for any part of Jewish life: learning, kids and games, home and family, shul and community, work and business, or productivity and tools (AI apps too).

Who owns my app?

You do. You can change the price, update it or take it down by emailing us.

Can I list a subscription or school-license app?

Yes, and listing it is free. You keep billing on your own system. Your listing links to your site with your own AppShuk link, and can show an AppShuk promo code. School and organization licenses also get a Request a quote button: the request goes to you by email, with AppShuk copied.

AppShuk earns a referral share on the customers it sends you, agreed with you in a simple one-page agreement.
