About AppShuk

AppShuk is a free directory of apps and websites made by our community, named for the shuk: many makers, one place to look.

People across the community are building useful apps: a teacher's brachos game, a parent's chore chart, a developer's zmanim tool, a shul's tzedakah page, an organization's event sign-up. Most of them are hard to find. AppShuk puts them in one place, so you can find one that's been checked.

The shuk has six sections: Learning & Education, Kids & Games, Home & Family, Shul & Community, Work & Business, and Productivity & Tools.

Anyone can list an app or website: teachers, parents, developers, and organizations. Every app is reviewed before it goes live.

AppShuk is a free directory. The Get app button takes you to the maker’s own site or store.

AppShuk is run by Binyamin Plotzker, a Torah educator who builds apps himself.
